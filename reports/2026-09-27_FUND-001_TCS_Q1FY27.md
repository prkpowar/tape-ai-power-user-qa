## Exact question

> Give the latest quarterly revenue, EBITDA, PAT and EPS for TCS. Show the reporting period and units for every number. Also state the observation/data freshness date if available.

Tape AI returned:

- Revenue: ₹72,275 Cr

## Independent verification

TCS's official investor calendar lists **09 Jul 2026** as the Q1 FY27 earnings release date, not 10 Jul 2026. The official Q1 release is dated 09 Jul 2026 and states that TCS reported consolidated IFRS results for the quarter ending 30 Jun 2026.

The detailed Q1 filing shows:

- Revenue from operations: ₹72,275 Cr
- Profit before exceptional items and tax: ₹18,612 Cr
- Profit before tax: ₹17,944 Cr
- Profit after tax / profit for the period: ₹13,420 Cr
- Basic and diluted EPS: ₹36.90

It also shows Q1 FY26 revenue of ₹63,437 Cr and profit for the period of ₹12,819 Cr.

TCS's investor FAQ separately reports Q1 FY2027 net income of ₹138,490 million (₹13,849 Cr) excluding exceptional items. This is a different basis from the ₹13,420 Cr profit-for-period figure, because the detailed filing includes the ₹668 Cr exceptional legal-claim item.

## What is correct

### Revenue

₹72,275 Cr is correct.

### PAT / profit for period

₹13,420 Cr is correct when PAT is interpreted as profit for the period after the exceptional item.

### EPS

₹36.90 is correct.

### Revenue YoY growth

13.9% is consistent with ₹72,275 Cr versus ₹63,437 Cr.

### Derived EBITDA / operating-profit figure

₹18,556 Cr is reproducible as a derived operating-profit/EBITDA-style figure from the operating-expense presentation commonly used by financial-data services. Tape AI explicitly told the user that this was a derived figure rather than a named TCS line.

**Classification: PASS / positive transparency behavior.**

## Confirmed finding

### TPQ-TIME-003 — Incorrect earnings-release date
**Severity: P2**

Tape AI stated that the Q1 FY27 results were declared on **10 Jul 2026**. TCS's official investor calendar and official Q1 FY27 result page show **09 Jul 2026**.

**Impact:** Low impact for an ordinary current snapshot, but material to provenance and historical/point-in-time testing.

**Suggested fix:** Prefer the issuer's official investor calendar/result-release date for the release date. Keep release date, filing date, period end date and data-refresh timestamp as separate metadata fields.

## Clarification / UX opportunity

### Derived-vs-reported metric presentation

Tape AI already disclosed that EBITDA was derived. That is good behavior, not a confirmed defect.

The product can nevertheless make the distinction even clearer by formatting the result as:

> Derived operating-profit/EBITDA proxy: ₹18,556 Cr  
> Basis: calculated from reported revenue and operating-expense components  
> Status: derived, not an explicitly reported TCS EBITDA line

This should be tracked as a usability improvement only if repeated tests show users can reasonably confuse the metric.

## Regression tests

1. Ask for the latest earnings release date and compare it with the issuer's official investor calendar.
2. For every requested metric that is not explicitly reported, require a visible "derived" label and calculation basis.
3. For PAT/net income, disclose whether exceptional items are included or excluded.
4. For historical research, keep publication/release date separate from data-refresh timestamp.

## Interview takeaway

The important QA behavior here is selective classification:

> I did not mark the response as broadly inaccurate. I independently reconciled the material financial values first. That showed the revenue, profit-for-period and EPS were correct. I then isolated one confirmed metadata defect — the release date was off by one day — and separated that from a presentation opportunity around derived EBITDA. This is how I would turn an AI answer into an evidence-based product test rather than a subjective review.
