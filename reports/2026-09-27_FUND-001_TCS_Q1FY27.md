## Findings

### TPQ-TIME-003 — Incorrect result-release date
**Severity: P2**

Tape AI stated the Q1 FY27 results were declared on **10 Jul 2026**. TCS's official calendar says the earnings release occurred on **09 Jul 2026**, and the official press release is dated July 9, 2026. citeturn238112search4turn301084view0

**Impact:** A one-day error is low impact for a normal snapshot, but it matters in historical/point-in-time research and source provenance.

**Suggested fix:** Prefer the issuer's investor calendar/press-release publication date for the official result-release date, and expose both result date and data-refresh date separately.

### TPQ-CALC-007 / TPQ-UX-005 — Derived EBITDA/operating-profit label needs stronger definition
**Severity: P2**

Tape AI correctly disclosed that TCS does not publish a line explicitly named "EBITDA" and that it derived ₹18,556 Cr from revenue and operating expenses. That is useful. However, users can still confuse this derived operating-profit/EBITDA proxy with TCS management's separately reported **24.0% operating margin** in the official IFRS release. citeturn301084view2

The ₹18,556 Cr figure is consistent with the common "sales minus operating expenses excluding depreciation/finance" presentation used by market-data sites, but it should be labelled explicitly as a **derived proxy**, not as a directly reported TCS line.

**Suggested fix:** Use wording such as:
> "Derived operating profit/EBITDA proxy: ₹18,556 Cr (calculated; not an explicitly reported EBITDA line). TCS reported operating margin separately at 24.0% under its IFRS presentation."

### TPQ-RES-003 — Single-source verification caveat
**Severity: P3**

Tape AI explicitly warned that the 13.9% growth figure had not been checked against a second source. This is good transparency, not itself a defect.

**Recommendation:** Preserve the caveat and, for critical benchmarks, add an independent source reconciliation step.

## Regression tests

1. Query any issuer's latest earnings and verify the release date against the issuer's investor calendar.
2. When a requested metric is derived, require the response to distinguish:
   - reported metric
   - derived metric
   - management metric
3. For PAT/net income, disclose whether exceptional items are included or excluded.
4. For historical research, preserve publication date separately from the data refresh timestamp.
